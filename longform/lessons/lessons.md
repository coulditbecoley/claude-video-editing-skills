# Lessons (mistakes that cost time, and the rule that prevents each one)

Add to this after every edit. Newest at the bottom.

## 2026-09-25 Asgard Agents Day 1

1. **Captions on long-form.** The first pass carried short-form habits over. Rule: long-form has no captions, and the short-form edit-video skill is off-limits for it.
2. **Clicking CapCut's timeline to seek trimmed two clips** and desynced 0:19-0:21.6. Rule: never click the timeline; check with `verify_draft.py` and the files.
3. **A loop variable named `H` shadowed the canvas height.** It built a 3840x1 canvas, and every export hung at 30% "Applying features". Rule: never reuse `W`/`H`. ccut now refuses resolutions under 16 px.
4. **pycapcut masks don't render in CapCut.** The facecam overlay showed full-frame and hid the zooms. Rule: pre-crop the facecam into its own clip and overlay that.
5. **Zooms ballooned the recorded facecam.** Rule: overlay the cropped cam on zoom pieces; aim zooms so the zoomed copy falls off-frame, or grow the overlay to cover it.
6. **Misplaced clicks on a multi-monitor desktop** opened Coley's other projects and created empty ones ("0925 (1)", "0925 (2)"). Rule: use per-monitor DPI-aware coordinates, confirm the title before clicking, and never move CapCut between monitors.
7. **New drafts only show after a CapCut restart**, and a draft rebuilt under a running CapCut may not open. Rule: build with CapCut fully quit.
8. **Exporting was Coley's job.** Rule: deliver the draft, never render.
9. **A transition before a 0.135 s piece was silently dropped** (the Paperclip to X swipe). Rule: leave at least the transition's duration on both sides, and diff the draft's transitions against the EDL.
10. **Loudnorm on mono, then pan to stereo, added +3 dB.** Rule: pan to stereo before loudnorm.

## 2026-09-29 Asgard Agents Day 6

1. **Zoom window edges landed mid-word** (117.8, 158.2, 458.3). Every piece join gets a 30 ms voice fade, so a mid-word split dips the audio. Rule: after `gen_edl.py`, list the contiguous joins and move any that aren't in a silence.
2. **Cards with a fixed centre overran the canvas** (wide chips and logo cards). Rule: anchor chips to the left margin and logo cards to the right margin, from the manifest widths.
3. **`capcut_grab.py` picked a minimized CapCut window** (-32000 rect), and a browser stayed on top. Rule: enumerate the CapCut windows, then ShowWindow(SW_RESTORE) and focus the visible one before any screenshot or click.
4. **`taskkill` without /F closed CapCut cleanly from the home screen** (no project open, no dialog). This is quicker than driving the menu when no draft is open.
5. **Stutters slipped through both Whisper passes** ("great, great", "it's it's", "that that"). Rule: add a disfluency-primed pass and diff two literal passes; A/B every cut in isolation.
6. **Transition edges clipped words**: a span ending within 0.1 s of the last word loses it under the flash and whoosh. Rule: >= 0.35 s tail and >= 0.25 s head at transitions, checked in code.
7. **Per-piece drift restarted at every join** and read as a glitch (5:59, and the choppy exit). Rule: one continuous camera path across pieces (ccut `path`).
8. **The cursor tracker's low scores (0.72-0.76) were glowing particles** on the Agents Window. Rule: keep hits >= 0.84 and spot-check a random contact sheet.
9. **A 30 ms voice fade on a split that isn't a cut dips the word.** ccut now skips fades on source-contiguous joins.

12. **A growing facecam overlay (cam_grow, up to 1.22x) read as the facecam changing size**, and it sometimes fell out of step with the screen. Rule: fixed overlay, with punch-in zooms where the facecam is in the shot.
13. **A slow push of any size shows a sliver of the recorded facecam beside a fixed overlay** (the copy shifts outward). Pushes only happen where the facecam isn't in the recording.

## 2026-10-07 AI Agents Business Ep 1: six Shorts (edit-shorts, Codex audit round)

1. **The full-width facecam put the mouth below the Shorts UI line (y 2880).** Codex caught it. Rule: crop the facecam with no headroom (top of the hair) and scale it so the mouth lands at about y 2830. Here that was a 734x486 crop at (2950,1518), scaled 2.73 and centred. Check against a red y=2880 line on the stills.
2. **Caption rules need enforcing after final tokenization.** A two-word fix ("affirm" to "a firm") counted as one token, so 5-word chunks got through and the gold landed on the wrong word. Rule: split fixes into tokens; merges stay at 4 words or fewer; every chunk gets one gold word (a keyword, otherwise the longest content word); keep GREEN LIGHT together; a lone "AND" joins its sentence.
3. **Captions showed words that weren't in the cut audio** ("SO", "OBVIOUSLY", "BASIS"). Whisper word times leak across edges. Rule: a word needs 0.12 s inside the piece (or to start at its edge) and must start 0.15 s before the out-point. Skip a word that an isolated snippet proves is mistimed.
4. **Cuts need a reason and an answer.** v1 ran 26-45 s; after the audit they run 20-34 s. Dropped: the interface tour ("pop over here"), restated rules, and a second story at the end. Added: the rule that explains the green light (the voice line, plus a card).
5. **Copy accuracy for Shorts:** "crashed" became "got stuck" (it was a workspace-validation failure), "5 paying customers" became "Goal:", and "Two AIs beat one" became "The second AI said no". Cards say only what the clip shows.
6. **Whooshes peaking on a loud first syllable clipped the mix (+0.1 dBFS).** Rule: peak whooshes 0.12 s before the cut at volume 0.2, and measure the full mix (voice, music and SFX) for every Short.
7. **`herdr wait` doesn't exist in this Herdr build.** Poll `herdr pane get` for idle or done instead.

## 2026-10-23 Asgard Agents Day 30

1. **CapCut snaps every clip start and every source trim to a whole frame when it opens a draft.** Peak-aligned SFX drifted up to 33 ms. A sub-frame head trim was re-snapped on open too. Rule: frame-align every SFX target (`q()`), use whole-frame hit offsets, pad each sample so its measured peak sits a whole number of frames in (`audio/pad_sfx.py`), and let ccut round starts to the nearest frame. Verify against CapCut's own `Timelines/<id>/draft_content.json` with `sfx_check.py`, never the root draft ccut wrote.
2. **Snapping cut edges to the nearest energy dip can pick the wrong gap.** Five joins clipped a word ("on thing", "they'm up", "a spot", "Did get", "is completed"). Rule: after every cuts change, re-stitch and re-hear each changed join in an isolated snippet of `qa_voice.wav`; mark an edge exact ("x") when the dip search lands inside a word.
3. **A snippet that starts mid-word reports the tail as a filler.** Two "uh" cuts (726.1, 776.0) removed "came" and "sponsorship bar". Rule: confirm a filler with a snippet that starts in silence before it.
4. **Whisper's default pass can hallucinate across a long pause.** It heard "a new account" in a 21 s silence; the literal snippet had "they did get back on X". Rule: a phrase that crosses a long silence comes from the literal pass or a snippet.
5. **A filler cut on a scrolling page jumps the picture.** Rule: put a framing change (1.0 <-> 1.5x) on exactly that cut so it reads as a camera cut, or keep the filler if no frame fits.
6. **At 1.5x a top-aligned frame still shows 18 px of the recorded facecam,** and `allowed()` then pulls the frame sideways (the Valhalla boxes fell off-frame). Rule: top-row zooms at 1.55x or more; `hl()` now refuses boxes that land off-frame and says so.
7. **The CapCut home window and editor both launch on the LG.** Coley wants nothing on the main screen. Rule: run `cc_keep_spectre.py` (15 ms watcher) before launching or opening a draft; it moves each window within a frame or two (Coley approved that flicker).
8. **Highlight boxes on a canvas he scrolls need per-moment coordinates and a motion guard.** Rule: measure from a gridded frame at each box's own moment; hold only while the page is still, min 1.5 s.
9. **SFX hit offsets were guessed, not read off the animation keys** (Codex). Rule: derive each sound's hit frame from ccut's actual keyframes (pop full size 154 ms = frame 5, glow settles 200 ms = frame 6, slide overshoot 220 ms = frame 7, a swap = frame 0).
10. **The asset splice inserted helper consts only once**, so a label change in `assets_day30.mjs` ("If approved") never reached the renderer. Rule: `splice_assets.py` now replaces the helper block on every run; check a changed card's PNG after rendering.
11. **A live page's side feeds can carry abuse and spam** (Pump.fun "Hot callouts" and chat). Rule: read every visible feed on a live platform page and mask unrelated feeds for the whole stretch with an opaque blurred, darkened patch.

12. **The OBS webcam picture lags the mic by about 100 ms, and afftdn delays the cleaned voice another 25 ms** (Coley: "the face cam and the audio speech do not line up"). Neither the container (all streams start at 0) nor ccut was at fault. Rule: measure lip sync on every new recording before editing: MediaPipe lip landmarks (inner-lip gap 13-14 over face height) vs voice loudness, both band-passed 2-8 Hz, pooled over several windows (`lipsync_mp.py`, `lip_scan.py`, `lip_analyze.py`). Mouth-darkness and frame-difference metrics gave r of about 0 (beard, head motion). Fix by shifting every segment in/out by +SYNC and delaying the voice file by SYNC, so all cut decisions (made on the voice) stay put.
13. **The display layout changed mid-session** (the Spectre moved from -3840,5 to -3782,2144; the primary went to 3840 wide). The hard-coded watcher then moved CapCut off-screen and fought Windows, flickering the main screen. Rule: `cc_keep_spectre.py` finds the left-most monitor at run time and gives up on a window after 3 moves; re-check the monitor list before any CapCut screen work.
14. **A cut that ends inside a pause can't be pause-collapsed** (the span then ends mid-silence), leaving a 1.3 s gap ("platform. ... So"). Rule: start a filler cut inside the preceding pause so about 0.22 s remains.
15. **The webcam lag changed mid-recording** (about 378 ms from 0:37 to source ~720 s, about 83 ms after; 78-90 ms in the camera shots). v3's single 75 ms shift came from pooling: the four windows at about -400 ms were dismissed as aliasing. Codex's independent v3 audit caught it. Rule: map lip sync over the WHOLE recording with sliding windows (`lip_timeline.py` on `probe/mouth_full.npy`) and give each section its own shift, with every section edge inside a cut or transition.
16. **An integer audio hop per video frame (16000 // 30 = 533 instead of 533.33) faked a steady 0.63 ms/s "drift".** Rule: index audio by round(i * SR / FPS), never by a cumulative integer hop.
17. **CapCut snaps picture and voice source starts to the 30 fps grid separately**, so a 353 ms picture-vs-voice offset became 333 or 367 piece by piece. Rule: every lip-sync shift is a whole number of frames (ccut `av_offset`), then it survives exactly; verify picture-minus-voice per piece in CapCut's Timelines copy.
18. **The display layout changed twice in one session** (to a 3-screen layout and back). Rule: the watcher re-reads the monitors every second, and every screen step re-checks EnumDisplayMonitors first.
19. **Highlight boxes measured on one frame drift when the picture is shifted for lip sync** (the treasury box sat 250 px high, Codex v4). Rule: `hl()` takes the measurement frame time (`mt`); if the shifted picture differs from it, the box shows when the picture IS that frame, and the stillness guard drops it otherwise. Re-render every canvas box after any sync change.

## 2026-10-24 AI Agents Business Ep 3 (PaperRows update)

1. **The webcam lag changed twice in one 10-minute take** (~100 ms, then ~403 ms from source ~214 to ~319 s, then ~100 ms again). Rule: always map the whole take before deciding a single shift; a step change is normal, not a measurement glitch.

Codex then rejected bilabial anchors that were picked "nearest the expected lag" as circular. Rule: report such a section as unverified instead of claiming a number; prefer the session's neighbouring measured sections and flag it to Coley for an eye check.

Rule: find when the page stops changing (frame diff vs the final layout), take the still from that frame, and hold the cover until the live picture reaches it.

6. **Highlight boxes need region-limited motion checks.** The whole-frame check dropped boxes on the Agents Window because of its animations elsewhere. `moves(t0, t1, rect)` now looks only at the box, waits up to 2 s for a scroll to settle, and refuses a box whose page moved since it was measured.

8. **The SFX checker hard-coded the previous project's draft name.** Fixed to read the project from `edit.edl.json`. Rule: every checker derives its target from the EDL, and QA files carry the EDL and voice hashes.
9. **A Whisper generator consumed twice prints nothing** (joincheck.py v1): materialise `segments` with `list()` before iterating twice.
10. **Narration that says "customers" where the screen says "checkout purchases":** cards carry the screen's word; the spoken line is Coley's call, so flag it to him rather than cutting the punchline.

## 2026-10-28 Asgard Agents Day 35

1. **Copying the SFX kit with `audio/*.wav` overwrote this video's cleaned voice with Ep 3's** (both are `voice-main.wav`). The filler sweep then ran on the wrong voice. Rule: copy the kit by name (pop.wav, sfx-*.wav, pad_sfx.py), never a glob, and check `voice-main.wav`'s duration equals the source before any voice-based step.
2. **CPU Whisper was starved** (other apps' Chrome plus the lip trackers): the small model hadn't finished one 10-minute chunk in 75 minutes. GPU faster-whisper (RTX 3070, medium.en, float16) ran the default pass, the literal pass and the filler sweep in about 10 minutes. Setup: a scratchpad venv with `faster-whisper nvidia-cublas-cu12 "nvidia-cudnn-cu12==9.*" "av<14"` and `gpuw_boot.py` adding the pip CUDA `bin` dirs to the DLL path. Rule: transcribe on the GPU.
3. **The MediaPipe mouth tracker:** decoding on the GPU with the crop done in the decoder (`-c:v hevc_cuvid -crop top x bottom x left x right`) is about 2x the CPU path and matches it frame-for-frame (r 0.998, zero offset). Buffering a 20-45 s chunk with `capture_output` ran three workers out of memory. Rule: stream frames from a `Popen` pipe one at a time; 20 s chunks, 3-4 workers.
4. **This take's webcam lag drifted, not stepped:** mostly ~85-100 ms or ~400-435 ms, but with strong in-between readings (317 ms r 0.50, 250 ms r 0.51, 183 ms r 0.66) and short islands, flipping several times in the first 4 minutes. A two-state model would have left some pieces ~250 ms off. Rule: `lip_units.py` gives every kept unit the whole-frame lag that maximises its own 60 fps mouth/voice correlation, smoothed across units by a Viterbi with a cost per 100 ms of change; edges sit in the cuts between units by construction. Short windows padded wide pick up their neighbours' lag, so pad only to 2.5 s.
5. **Claude in Chrome put its tab group in Coley's main Brave window** (background tab), and another Claude session then took that tab over. Rule: ElevenLabs via the browser only when his main browser is free; otherwise use the series' own beds and say so.
6. **`capcut_drive.py dblclick` is two single clicks 0.55 s apart** (uiautomation waits after each `Click`), so it only selects a home tile. Rule: open a draft with the skill's `capcut_click.py dbl X Y` (real double-click), after a short idle pause.
7. **pycapcut sets a sped-up clip's length to `round(source_us / speed)`**, so a speed rounded to 4 decimals overran the next clip by 1 µs and the build failed (SegmentOverlap). Rule: a fast-forward's output is a whole number of milliseconds and its speed is derived from that exactly, after the lip-sync shift has rounded in/out.
8. **Canvas boxes measured at phrase start + 0.4 s were pre-scroll:** he scrolls one step right as he starts reading a card. Rule: check motion inside the box region first, measure on a frame after the scroll settles, and pass that frame time as `mt`. Fixed UI on a page that doesn't scroll (the Agents Window) takes `fixed=True` (its fire animations and the cursor otherwise fail the stillness check).
9. **A 50-80 ms gap inside "Discord" is its /k/ closure, not a pause:** a literal pass heard "Discord Discord" and cutting the "gap" removed "cord". Rule: verify any doubled word with an isolated snippet of the source before cutting; a short silence inside a word is a stop consonant.
10. **A cut that started at the end of a pause left a 5.5 s gap in the hook** (Day 30 #14 again: a pause partly outside a span isn't collapsed). Rule: a cut after a pause starts inside it.
11. **Pauses he scrolls through:** cutting them jumps the page; keeping them leaves dead air. Rule: the moving part of a canvas pause over 0.55 s plays as a muted fast-forward of about 0.35 s (1.7-4.6x), the still part is cut. On the 3D World and X the camera/page never settles, so a pause over 0.5 s is cut and the framing flips (1.0 / 1.5x) on that cut.
12. **A page that blanks right after a click sits under the last word** when the lip shift is large (0.47 s here): the X buyback click. Rule: scan each piece's first and last frames for loading/blank pictures and cover them with a still of the settled page (Ep 3 #4/#5), or end the piece before the page changes.
